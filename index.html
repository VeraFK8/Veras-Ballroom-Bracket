✦ KNOW THE POINTS ✦

SCORING SYSTEM

Your fantasy team, weekly predictions, preseason bracket, and loyalty bonus all feed the same season total.

FANTASY TEAM

Judges score ÷ 2 (rounded) = base fantasy points each week.

+10 Highest scoring couple +7 Second highest +5 Third highest +8 Perfect score +5 First 10 of the season +5 Challenge / relay win +2 Safe -3 Bottom group -8 Eliminated

WEEKLY PICKS

Make four predictions before the Tuesday 7 PM CT lock.

+10 Correct highest scorer +5 Correct lowest scorer +15 Correct eliminated couple +5 Correct perfect-score yes/no

PRESEASON BRACKET

Predict the season from the Top 8 all the way to the Mirrorball.

+3 Each correct Top 8 +5 Each correct Top 6 +8 Each correct Top 4 +12 Each correct Final 3 +20 Correct runner-up +40 Correct winner

FINALE + LOYALTY

Your fantasy couples can earn major placement bonuses at the end.

+10 Makes semifinals +15 Makes finale +20 3rd place +30 Runner-up +50 Mirrorball winner +20 Keep original four all season

MIDSEASON REROLL ♡

After Week 5, every player gets one chance to change their fantasy lineup.

1× One reroll per season W6+ Opens after Week 5 KEEP All points already earned NEW New lineup scores going forward TRADEOFF Using it gives up the +20 loyalty bonus

Once a couple is eliminated, they stay visible in your lineup history but stop earning future fantasy points.
